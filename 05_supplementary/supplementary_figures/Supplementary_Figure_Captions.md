# Supplementary Figure Captions

## Figure S1. Instability reveals masked magnitude divergence in DES.

**Left:** Relationship between absolute pooled effect size and instability score. Genes with low pooled effect size can exhibit high instability, indicating masking of subgroup-specific responses.

**Right:** Effect divergence across instability tiers (background, top 10%, top 5%). High-instability genes show substantially increased divergence despite weak pooled effects. Masked gene fraction quantifies pooling-induced signal loss.
